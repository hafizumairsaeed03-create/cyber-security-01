Module 1: Ethical Hacking Kya Hai? (A to Z Guide)

Is lesson mein hum bilkul shuruat se samjhenge ke Ethical Hacking kya hoti hai, kis ke liye hoti hai, aur yeh kyun zaroori hai.

📌 Step 1: Ethical Hacking Kya Hai? (Definition)
Ethical Hacking ka matlab hai kisi website, system, ya network mein Ijazat (Permission) le kar security ki khamiyan (vulnerabilities) dhoondna.

Iska maqsad system ko nuksan pahunchana nahi hota, balki chhor ya bad-neeyat hackers (Black Hat) se pehle un khamiyon ko dhoond kar band (patch) karna hota hai.
👤 Step 2: Hackers Ki 3 Badi Qismein (Types)
Hacking ki duniya mein 3 tarah ke hackers hote hain:
  • 1. White Hat Hacker (Ethical Hacker): Yeh achhe log hote hain jo permission le kar security test karte hain aur system ko secure banate hain.
  • 2. Black Hat Hacker (Cracker): Yeh bina ijazat ke system mein ghaste hain, data chori karte hain, aur nuksan pahunchate hain.
  • 3. Grey Hat Hacker: Yeh na poore achhe hote hain na poore buray. Yeh bina permission ke bugs dhoondte hain lekin nuksan nahi pahunchate, balki reward ya fame chahte hain.
🎯 Step 3: Ethical Hacking Kis Ke Liye Istemal Hoti Hai?
Ethical Hacking aam taur par in cheezon ke liye istamal hoti hai:
  • 1. Company Data Defense: Banks, Google, Facebook jaisi companies apne user data ko chori hone se bachane ke liye Ethical Hackers ko hire karti hain.
  • 2. Bug Bounty Programs: Barri companies logon ko dawat deti hain ke un ke system mein khami dhoond kar batayein aur hazaron dollars inam jeetein.
  • 3. Government & Military Security: Mulk ke defense systems aur government websites ko cyber attack se bachane ke liye.
  • 4. Penetration Testing: Kisi bhi nayi website ya app ko launch karne se pehle us par attack kar ke uski taqat check karna.
🚀 Step 4: Yeh Course Kisse Seekhna Chahiye? (Who Should Learn)
Yeh course un sab logon ke liye hai jo:
  • Cyber Security field mein apna career banana chahte hain.
  • Apni website ya application ko secure karna chahte hain.
  • Bug Bounty karke online earning karna chahte hain.
  • Hacking aur Technology ki gahrai (depth) ko samajhna chahte hain.
🛠️ Step 5: Essential Tools Jo Ethical Hackers Istemal Karte Hain
Ethical Hacking mein yeh tools sab se zyada istemal hote hain:
  • Kali Linux: Hacking ke liye bana hua special Operating System.
  • Nmap: Networks aur Open Ports scan karne ke liye.
  • Burp Suite: Web Applications (Websites) par bugs dhoondne ke liye.
  • Metasploit: System vulnerabilities test karne ke liye.
⚠️ Important Note: Bina kisi ki permission ke unke system ya network par hacking try karna Cyber Crime hai. Is course ka maqsad sirf aur sirf Defence aur Educational Learning hai.
